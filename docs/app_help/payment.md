# Payment

Settings — Payment tab. Toggle: Supplier Payment | Customer Payment.

## Supplier Payment (inline)
Supplier, Outstanding Due, Amount Paid, Payment Mode (Cash/Online/Cheque/NEFT/RTGS/UPI), Date, Reference.
Save Payment (F5), Clear, Delete Selected. Payment History tree below.

## Customer Payment (inline)
Customer, Outstanding Due/Credit, Cash and Online amounts, Date, Reference.
Save Payment (F5). History: double-click to Edit Payment.
