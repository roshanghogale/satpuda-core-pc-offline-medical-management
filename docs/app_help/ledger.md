# Ledger

Settings — Ledger tab. Toggle: Supplier Ledger | Customer Ledger.

## Supplier Ledger
Supplier Name, From/To dates, View Report, Reset.
Tree: Date, Type, Reference, Details, Amount, Paid, Due, Credit, Running Balance.
Summary: Total Purchase, Total Paid, Total Returns, Balance Due.

## Customer Ledger
Same layout for customers. Includes sales, returns, and standalone customer payments.
