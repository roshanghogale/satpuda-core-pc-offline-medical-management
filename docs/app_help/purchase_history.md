# Purchase History

## Filter Options
From Date, To Date, Fin Year, Supplier, Status (Due/Credit/Paid), Schedule, Sort By.
Apply Filter for dates.

## Actions
Edit Purchase (fullscreen, Update Purchase saves). No separate View-only dialog.
Delete Purchase — reduces stock, cannot undo.

Columns include Paid at Entry, Paid via Payment, Entry Due, Status.

## Export
Current View, Purchase Register, Monthly Summary, Supplier Due Report, GST Purchase Report.
