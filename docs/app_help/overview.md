# Satpuda Core overview

Pharmacy and veterinary billing and stock management for Windows.

## Top navigation
| Screen | Purpose |
| Home | Dashboard, quick actions, stats |
| Sales | Customer invoices (billing) |
| Purchase | Supplier stock-in |
| Inventory | Stock, batches, expiry |
| Sales History | Past sales, print, export |
| Purchase History | Past purchases, supplier due |
| Returns | Sales return, purchase return, write-off |
| Settings | All configuration |

## Satpuda AI vs Satpuda voice
| Satpuda AI | Read-only tutor (Gemini). ? button bottom-right. Explains screens and steps. |
| Satpuda | Voice navigation (microphone). Opens screens only. Link inside Satpuda AI header. |

## Data safety
Satpuda AI never saves bills, stock, or settings. You perform all actions in the app UI.

See navigation_flow.md for when customers, suppliers, and stock are actually saved.
